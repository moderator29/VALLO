# Money copy sweep: protected, held and escrow promises

Branch `platform-grade-1-readiness`, swept 9 November 2026. Read-only sweep; this file is the only change.

## Summary

**Lead's correction, checked live 9 November 2026:** `payments_payluk_on` is TRUE in the live database (since 8 October 2026), not false as the seed says. So the class B items are off only if no Payluk key is set in Vercel production, which the repository cannot see; zero arrangements have ever been opened. `supabase/migrations/pending/payluk_switch_off_2026-11-09.sql` turns the switch off, and it heads the founder's SQL block.

The protected payment rail (Payluk, "escrow", "protected payment") is switched off: `payments_payluk_on` is seeded `false` by `supabase/migrations/20261007154344_d77_founder_rulings_on_the_gate.sql:354-357` (`on conflict do nothing`), no later migration sets it true, `flagIsOn` (`apps/web/src/lib/flags/read.ts:11-21`) fails closed, and `providerEnabled("payluk")` (`apps/web/src/lib/payments/providers/kill-switch.ts:25-30`) runs Payluk only while that row is explicitly true and `PAYMENTS_KILL_PAYLUK` is not `1`. `escrowRailLive()` and `memberWalletRailLive()` (`apps/web/src/lib/payments/providers/index.ts:45-56`) and `protectedRailLive()` (`apps/web/src/lib/money/rail-live.ts`) all read that switch plus a configured Payluk key. I confirmed the default from code and migrations only, not from the live database; one read settles it: `select key, enabled from public.feature_flags where key = 'payments_payluk_on';`.

Even so, a lot of member copy describes escrow as a choice members have today. It is printed without any rail check on the public docs, help, safety, supply and landing pages, in the support FAQ and in both assistant prompts. It also shows on the payment step itself, where the rail-off branch still says "Escrow is paused". I also counted the retired Vallo Guarantee (D51) where it is still promised as cover for new payments, mainly Terms section 14. Counts:

- **Class A (false promise shown today): 62 items.** About 45 are escrow or held-money promises and 17 are Guarantee or implied-cover lines. Most of them flow from 12 constants: `NO_CUSTODY_SENTENCE`, `PAYMENT_GATE_SENTENCE`, `PAYOUT_ANSWER`, the `docs/facts.ts` set and the `payChoice` paused lines. Fixing those constants fixes most of the screens.
- **Class B (gated off today): 24 items.** All sit behind `payments_payluk_on` (by way of `protectedRailLive`, `escrowRailLive` or `memberWalletRailLive`), behind a provider arrangement that can only exist once Payluk reports one, behind `money_policy.guarantee_bps = 0`, or in a component nothing imports.
- **Class C (true and fine): 38 locations.**
- **Class D (internal, admin, dev or retired SQL): 40 locations or groups.**

Gates every proposal below was written to pass:
- `apps/web/src/lib/copy/custody-words.test.ts`: no "your wallet", "Open escrow", or bare "wallet" in member copy except the allowlisted Wallet screen names.
- `apps/web/src/lib/copy/no-payment-balance.test.ts:29`: never "keeps no balance", "no wallet", "nothing to withdraw", or "wallet, balance or escrow".
- `apps/web/src/lib/trust/claims.ts`: no un-negated safe, secure, protected, guaranteed or verified.
- `apps/web/src/lib/copy/banned-phrases.ts`: no schedule promises. That is why I write "not open yet" and never "opens soon". "Opens soon" is not matched literally, but it is the same promise as the banned "opening soon" and "available soon".
- `apps/web/scripts/check-valuation-words.mjs`: no valuation words (none of these lines touch them).
- No em dashes in any proposal.

Naming Paystack: `lib/money/copy.ts` and `docs/facts.ts` both have a rule that no provider is named outside receipts and legal pages. The founder's brief names Paystack. Every proposal below says "Paystack". To keep the no-name rule, read "our licensed payment processor" wherever "Paystack" appears.

Rent: the brief says rent has everything except the payment step. The approved rent line is "Payment for rentals is not open on Vallo yet. You can view, inspect and agree terms now." `apps/web/src/components/site/landing/hero-facts.test.ts:36` pins `PAYMENT_GATE_SENTENCE` to `/as soon as .*both of you confirm the agreement/`, so changing that sentence means updating the test too.
