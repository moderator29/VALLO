# AUDIT, NOVEMBER 2026 (PLATFORM GRADE 1, READ-ONLY PASS)

Written 7 November 2026 on `platform-grade-1-readiness`, from a read of the
code and the live database (Supabase `uccixoonmbhrnyczyigt`: 30 profiles, 755
migrations). Paths are under `apps/web/src/` unless they start with `docs/`,
`packages/` or `supabase/`. Three columns per area: what exists, what is
broken or unfinished, what is duplicated.

The headline: **Vallo is not missing a design system. It has too many layers
of one.** About 60 stylesheets are imported by `app/globals.css` (Track M,
section 17, brand glass, nav island, edge, spark, light, pixel, clean-17...),
each answering the last by position. The Grade 1 work is therefore mostly
CONSOLIDATION and STRUCTURE (one sheet, one banner, one step frame, one page
frame, thinner icons, calmer surfaces), not invention.

## 1. Shell and navigation

**Exists.** `(app)/layout.tsx` mounts `components/app/AppShell.tsx` (472
lines): desktop rail and a hand-rolled mobile drawer (both `AppRail`), a
sticky navy header (menu, compose pencil, bell on five paths only), and
`ShellDock` (`MobileTabBar`). Bottom tabs: Home (or Stays), Search, a centre
"More" tray (`DockMore`), Feed `/around` (Agreements when social is off),
Profile; plus a round "+" (`CreateDock`). Side nav model
`components/app/nav-model.ts`: Account (Plans, Messages, Notifications,
Saved, Agreements, Passport, AI Assistant), Money (Balance, Rewards, Invite),
Price check, Workspaces (Agent Mode, Host, Console), tail (Add a workspace,
Help, Pro, Settings). `SideSwitch` flips Property and Stays.

**Role switching: three mechanisms.** `ProfileSwitcher` sheet (opened by an
event from the profile's `SwitchRoleRow`), `ModeSwitcher` (only inside the
agent shell), `RoleSwitcher` (signed-out profile). Cookies `nf_mode`
(personal or working) and `nf_workspace`. No "agent tools" switch in the
member side nav itself; Agent Mode is just a link.

**Broken or stale.** `AppShell.tsx:403` says the round "+" is gone; it still
renders, so two triggers open one sheet. `MobileTabBar.tsx:308` says the
centre is the switch; it is `DockMore`. `nav-model.ts:27` says eleven rows;
there are 18 signed in. Dead CSS in `app/side-nav.css` (906 lines).

**Duplicated.** DockMore repeats 8 drawer rows. Agreements appears in up to
three places. Three drawers (AppShell inline, `WorkspaceDrawer`,
`HostDrawer`).

**Size.** Pages: `(app)` 119, `(auth)` 12, `(site)` 32, admin 52, agent 26,
host 17, `(dev)` 294 previews, single pages for welcome, offline, first-run.
55 API routes.

## 2. Design tokens and CSS

**Exists.** `packages/design-tokens/src/tokens.css` (4,752 lines, 913
`--nf-*`). Radii xs 6, sm 10, md 14, lg 18, xl 22, island 28, 2xl 32, pill.
Spacing 2, 4, 8, 12, 16, 24, 32, 48, 64, 96, 128. Type display, h0 to h4,
body-lg, body, body-sm, caption, overline, label, figure. Durations instant
90, press 120, fast 160, pop 180, base 240, slow 380, entrance 520,
deliberate 620, cinematic 900; eases standard, entrance, whip, exit, spring,
press. Motion setting (cinematic, standard, calm, off) in
`css/motion-pref.css`. Dark is the default theme; light was removed on 23
September and brought back on the 25th (`css/light.css`).

**Founder rulings still in force (keep them).** Text buttons are soft-cornered
rectangles, never pills (30 September, `docs/design/CLEAN_UNIFIED_DIRECTION.md`
sections 17 and 19, enforced by `scripts/check-css-tokens.mjs`). Blue-led with
orange "spark" in a closed list of places. Heights 44 / 48 / 56 only.

**Broken or inconsistent.** 60 stylesheets layered by import position
(`app/globals.css`), several whose header comments contradict the import list
(the light.css note). 33 raw `text-[Npx]`. Motion curves duplicated in JS
(`lib/motion/ease.ts`). `scripts/check-tti.mjs` is wired nowhere.

**Gates that exist.** `check-css-tokens` (tokens, raw colours, capsules),
`check-no-em-dash`, `check-valuation-words`, `check-weight`,
`check-landing-height`, `check-security-headers`, `check-script-nonces`,
`check-deep-links` (cap sync only).

## 3. Icons

**Exists.** `design-system/icons/UiIcon.tsx`: 143 line glyphs (Lucide paths,
copied in) and 50 filled twins; sizes 12 to 40. Two strokes: lean (default,
1.25 to 2.25) and bold (1.5 to 3). The navigation chrome is switched to BOLD
(`css/symbols.css`, the 29 September "pump.fun" ruling) and the dock draws
`filled weight="bold"`. `BrandIcon` (144 3D clay objects), 64 PNG glyphs.

**Gap against the brief.** The founder now asks for "slimmer, cleaner icons ...
thin and elegant line icons, consistent stroke weights". The bold chrome is
the single biggest reason the shell looks heavier than the references.

**Duplicated.** Parallel stroke components: `SettingsGlyph`, `LineGlyph`,
`AdminGlyph`. 48 product files draw inline `<svg>`.

## 4. Feedback (toasts, sheets, steppers, headers)

**Toasts: two APIs.** Global store `lib/ui/toast.ts` (`toast.success`,
`toast.error`, dwell 2.4s and 6s) drawn by `ToastHost` inside `DetailsHost`:
15 call sites in 6 files. Legacy `components/ui/Toast.tsx` `useToast` (4
files). Most actions in the product show NO success feedback at all; many
show an inline sentence instead. This is the P0.4 gap.

**Other feedback.** `ConnectionLine` (online, offline, kept, slow, back:
brief and in place, as the founder ruled), `SuccessFlagHost` and
`SuccessSheet` for account moments (20 files), `StateMoment`, `ResultSheet`,
`RefusalHost`. `docs/SUCCESS_MOMENTS.md`.

**Sheets.** `components/ui/Sheet.tsx` (679 lines, detents, full page, side on
wide, footer) in about 85 files. That is the platform sheet; it should be
upgraded, not replaced. Duplicates: a second `Sheet` in
`components/app/account/rows.tsx:503`, two `ReportSheet`s, `DocumentSheet`,
`ActionSheetIllustrated`, `ExternalLinkSheet`, `FilterDrawer`, and 12 files
with hand-rolled `role="dialog"` (a guard test lets that list only shrink).

**Steppers.** No shared one: `StaysStepper`, `StepPath`, `PaymentSteps`,
`SegmentedProgress`, `Tracker`, plus each wizard's own. This is why the
listing wizard, KYC and host flows all feel different.

**Page frame.** `PageHeader` (103 files, `large` folds into the shell header)
and `Screen.tsx` helpers; also `WorkspaceHeader`, `SiteBackBar`,
`AuthBackBar` and page-specific headers.

## 5. Offline

**Exists, and it is strong.** `public/sw.js` (1,659 lines, cache v8): kept
pages per viewer (7 days, 60 pages), network race at 1.2s, `/offline`
fallback, images cache-first (200), Save-Data honoured, a long never-keep
list (money, messages, admin, agent, auth). `lib/offline/*`: outbox,
send-or-keep, replay guard, retry, shelf (IndexedDB shortlist), inspection
packs, viewer stamp. `components/app/OfflineTray.tsx`. `DeployWatch`
reloads phones onto new builds. `/offline` page with packs and shelf.

**Gaps.** `OfflineTray` is mounted only in the member layout (not agent, host
or admin). The agent side is never kept by the service worker, so the
founder's "switch on low or no data" cannot work for the agent side today.
No "Saved on your device, price may have changed" stamp on kept listings.

## 6. Native (Capacitor 8)

**Config.** `capacitor.config.ts`: `com.vallospaces.app`, loads the live
site, status bar DARK on `#010118`, not overlaying, keyboard resize Native,
splash held until the app paints. Plugins: app, browser, camera, haptics,
keyboard, push, share, splash, status bar; native Sign in with Apple and a
widget.

**`lib/native/*`.** Splash hide, status bar style from the THEME only (no
per-screen style), keyboard inset variable and scroll-into-view, Android back
(close overlay, pop a step, exit on a root), external links in the system
browser, deep links (`appUrlOpen`), push taps, share bridge, haptics in
`lib/ui/feedback.ts` (50 files; `warning` never used). 150 safe-area uses.

**Gaps.** No app lifecycle listener (resume refresh) outside calls. Status
bar does not follow light screens inside a dark header. `nativeHaptic` in
`lib/native/device.ts` is dead code.

## 7. Identity verification

**Exists.** One route `(app)/verification` with `KycFlow` (604 lines): ID
document, live selfie (oval guide, front camera, fallback to file), address
document, business question and details, consent, review. Admin desk
`app/admin/kyc` with ID beside selfie. Dojah and vNIN parked behind flags.

**Gaps against the brief.** Not a stepper of full pages; no country step, no
document cards, no capture-and-review with zoom and retake, no intro "what
will happen". **KYC is not required to submit or publish a listing**, nor for
a booking or payout (only crypto checks it). Stale step-count comment.

**Duplicated.** Identity documents are uploaded from four places (verification,
ApplyWizard, Owner/Agent/Firm register forms, HostWizard) and there are two
verification pages (`/verification`, `/agent/verification`).

## 8. Listing wizard

**Exists.** `app/agent/list/ListingWizard.tsx` (4,733 lines), 8 steps
(basics, photos, location, amenities, utilities, pricing, guest view,
submit), server draft on every step plus a device copy, segmented progress
with jump-back, media rules (3 minimum, 1600px), description 40 words. Paste
a WhatsApp broadcast to prefill (AI). Charges at the door and the SCUML
mandate are separate pages, enforced only at admin publish.

**Gaps.** Several questions per screen; no "Saved" tick in a top bar; no
"Check your listing" review with Change pills and "things to fix"; no AI
description writer with consent and undo; no property-type tile grid; no
full-screen pin picker; extra charges not in the wizard. A 4,733-line file is
the clearest single "no proper plan" smell in the codebase.

## 9. Messages

**Exists.** Inbox, thread (`ThreadView.tsx`, 1,779 lines), first message,
share; reply, forward, delete; typing indicator (broadcast); sending, failed
and waiting states; read ticks from `read_at`; calls on LiveKit (flagged).

**Gaps.** Composer: photo button, one-line input, send arrow. **No voice
recording at all** (a player exists with no data; the attachment bucket
allows only images and PDF). **No message reactions** (no table). "Delivered"
never shows (no column). No Report item on a message bubble. The assistant is
not in the inbox.

**Duplicated.** Four thread readers (messages, support messages, post, venue
messages); a separate agent inbox.

## 10. Assistant and support

**Exists.** `/assistant` (`AssistantChat`, streaming route, tools, consent,
proposals); a workspace copy in `app/agent/assistant`. `/support`
(`SupportChat`, an AI grounded in `lib/support/faq.ts` that escalates to a
real `support_tickets` row), `/support/new`, `/support/messages`. Staff desk
`/admin/support`.

**Gaps.** No pinned assistant conversation in Messages; the human support
chat is not reachable from inside the assistant as an inner page. `/support`
and `/settings/help` both embed SupportChat. Two help stores
(`help-articles.ts`, `faq.ts`).

## 11. Reporting

**Exists.** One `reports` table; `reportSomething` for listing, conversation,
message, review, business, event; social reports for post, story comment,
profile. Admin `/admin/queue?tab=reports`, flags and held lanes (moderation
scope).

**Gaps.** No Report on a message bubble, a photo or a transaction; a person
is reportable only as a social profile; transactions go through a support
form. Every report lands in one moderation queue rather than the right desk.
Two `ReportSheet` components.

## 12. Admin

**Exists.** 40+ desks under `app/admin` (kyc, listings, queue, money,
support, staff, oversight, compliance, switches...). Staff are added by
account at `/admin/staff` with `staff_grants` (scopes: listing_approval,
kyc_review, moderation, support, agreements, guarantee, finance, compliance,
operations), security keys and handbook acknowledgement.

**Gaps.** No business-developer onboarding desk (help a person met in real
life finish verification and a first listing). No social media manager desk:
`/admin/social` only moderates places.

## 13. Help centre and map

**Help.** `(site)/help`, `(site)/guides` (7 articles), `(site)/docs/[slug]`,
in-app `/support` and `/settings/help`. No article anatomy with screenshots,
tips and "Before you start".

**Map.** Leaflet with CARTO tiles (non-commercial terms, flagged in
`lib/maps/tiles.ts`) or MapTiler when a key is set. CARTO's terms are a real
launch risk; a free-to-use vector option needs the founder's sign-off on any
key.

## 14. Agent side

**Exists.** Dashboard (`RealDashboard`, `KycBanner`), listings workspace,
bookings, inspections with a visit calendar, earnings, analytics, firm,
portfolio, messages, notifications, reviews, settings, verification,
per-listing arrival, mandate, calendar, health, promotion.

**Gaps.** No "What do you manage?" first-entry sheet (the closest is "Add a
workspace"). No single Activity inbox (bookings, applications, viewings with
counts). No month calendar with legend counts across listings.

## 15. The order this audit implies

1. Shared pieces first (banner, sheet upgrade, step frame, page frame), so
   every later screen is built from them.
2. Thin chrome icons and calmer surfaces in the tokens: the cheapest change
   with the biggest visual effect across all pages.
3. Verification as a stepper, and required before deals.
4. Listing wizard re-flow (and splitting its 4,733-line file by step).
5. Messages: composer, voice notes (needs a storage bucket change), reactions
   (needs a table), assistant pinned in the inbox.
6. Agent side: switch in the side nav, kept for offline, What do you manage,
   Activity, Calendar.
7. Reporting routed to desks; the two new admin desks.
8. Help centre with real screenshots, after the screens are final.
