# Video One, "How Vallo works" (November 2026 cut): ElevenLabs recording brief

The new Video One is built on the upgraded platform (new dock, Vallo Circle,
Vallo Roommates, the whole move-in cost, compare, the assistant, stays and
tables, plus homes for sale, split the total, and room/menu details). It is
one vertical film, about **85 to 90 seconds**, and it has one job: make a
Nigerian viewer want to open Vallo today.

The picture is already built on a **provisional clock** laid out from the
script alone (`films/how2/timeline.json`, 20 lines, ~193 words, 87.7 s). When
your recording lands, the film re-anchors itself: nothing in it refers to a
number of seconds, every beat hangs on a line or a word of the voice. So the
one rule is the same as for the short films: **read every line as written and
do not re-cut it.** A re-worded line moves the beat that hangs on it.

The script to paste is `docs/marketing/voiceover/scripts/video-1-how-vallo-works.txt`
(one sentence per line, 20 lines). It is repeated at the bottom of this page.

Record it as **one take, one file**. Leave about a second of room tone at the

## How to say it

- **Voice:** Nigerian English, warm, unhurried, certain. A person explaining
  something useful to someone they like, not an advert voice. Smile on "Meet
  Vallo." and on "Download Vallo today."
- **Pace:** the film was timed at about **2.95 words a second** with a short
  breath (about a third of a second) between lines. Do not rush the two number
  lines (5 and 6), the buy line (7) or the split line (9), and do not drag
  the short ones (2, 16, 17, 19).
- **Shape:** come **down** at the end of every sentence, never up (except the
  real questions: "Want to buy instead?", "Got a question?", "Going away?",
  "Living with someone?", "Hungry?", which lift a little on their last word
  and then settle).
- **Breaths:** the commas are breaths, not dramatic pauses. Between line 1 and
  line 2 leave a clear half-second: the film cuts there and the phone arrives
  on "Meet Vallo."
- **The close:** lines 19 and 20 are the ask and the signature. Say "Download
  Vallo today." with a smile, then a real pause (about a second), then
  "Vallo. Real estate, done right." slower and lower: the music's logo hit
  lands on "Vallo."

## What the film deliberately does NOT say

- Nothing about paying rent through Vallo, refunds, escrow or a wallet:
  rental payment is not open yet, so no line promises a payment or a refund.
  Line 5 says only that the deposit is "refundable", the listing's own word.
- "Verified" is never said about a home. Line 16 is about people's badges.
- No count of users, no rating, no "best" or "cheapest", no promise word
  (safe, secure, protected, guaranteed).
- No fact needed that is not already in the repo. One item to confirm before
  you record: line 19 ("Download Vallo today.") assumes the apps are live
  in both stores, as the end card says.
